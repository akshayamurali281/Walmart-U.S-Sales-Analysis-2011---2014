# 🛒 Walmart U.S. Sales Analysis (2011–2014)

## 📑 Executive Summary
* **Scope:** Evaluates 4 years of transactional sales performance (2011–2014) across Walmart US retail operations.
* **Tech Stack:** Cleaned raw transactional data in **Excel** & **Python**, queried data models via **MySQL**, and built an interactive dashboard in **Power BI**.
* **Analytical Focus:** Isolates regional profitability drivers, identifies underperforming product categories, and projects key revenue metrics.

---

## 📊 Dataset Facts & KPI Highlights
* **Total Sales Analyzed:** $725.5K
* **Total Profit:** $108.4K
* **Total Operational Cost:** $617K
* **Overall Profit Margin:** 14.9%
* **Average Shipping Days:** 3.9 Days

---

## ❓ Problem Statement / Core Goal
Identify operational cost bottlenecks and evaluate category-level profit trends to optimize product strategies and regional allocation.

---

## 💡 Key Business Recommendations & Insights
* **Top Profit Contributor:** Seattle leads as the highest profit-contributing city, generating **$30K** in net profit.
* **Top Categories by Profit:** Copiers, Accessories, and Binders represent the most lucrative product lines.
* **Loss-Making Categories:** Machines and Bookcases currently operate at a net loss.
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Walmart should re-invest capital into high-profit cities on a scaled basis while overhauling marketing, pricing, and product strategies for loss-generating segments.
